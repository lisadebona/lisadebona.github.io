Our service

A clear path from the call to the drop-off.

Tell us where you are and what you are driving. We send the truck that fits the job and stay with it until the vehicle is moved safely.

  1. 01

    Call

    Reach us at (432) 283-9048. Share your location, the vehicle, and what happened.

  2. 02

    Dispatch

    We line up the right truck. Response time depends on where you are and how many calls are ahead of you.

  3. 03

    Recover

    The crew secures the vehicle and, after an accident, works to get the roadway clear.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    We transport it to your destination, the shop, or our yard for repairs and storage.

While you wait

If you can, move to a safe spot away from traffic, turn on your hazard lights, and stay inside the vehicle when it is unsafe outside.
